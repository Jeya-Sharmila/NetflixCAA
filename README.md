## Overview

Netflix is a global subscription video-on-demand (SVOD) service. This project examines the marketing decisions behind its growth, from product mix and packaging to pricing tiers, subscription distribution and a multi-channel promotion strategy that includes regional and celebrity-led campaigns.

## Topics Covered

### 1. Product Decisions
- **Product mix:** streaming of movies and TV shows, Netflix Originals, international content with subtitles and dubbing, kids and family section, interactive shows, mobile games and offline downloads
- **Product lines:** Movies, TV Series, Netflix Originals (e.g. Stranger Things, The Crown, Money Heist), Kids & Animation, Mobile Games
- **Product decisions:** investment in originals, localization, AI-driven personalized recommendations, multiple subscription plans, continuous innovation
- **Packaging (digital):** user-friendly interface, thumbnails and posters, preview trailers and autoplay, categories such as Trending and New, consistent design across devices
- **Labelling:** genre labels, age ratings, content tags (Top 10, Recommended for You), language information, content-type labels

### 2. Branding Decisions
- Original content strategy, starting with *House of Cards* in 2013
- Data-driven personalization
- Global-local approach to campaigns
- Story-first marketing
- Visual identity: Netflix red palette and the minimalist "N" logo

### 3. Pricing
- **Policies:** subscription-based, tiered and value-based pricing
- **Strategies:** penetration pricing, segmented pricing, psychological pricing (e.g. ₹199), and bundling with telecom and internet plans

### 4. Distribution
- Subscription-based (SVOD) model with plans based on video quality and number of screens
- Streaming across multiple devices
- 260+ million paid subscribers worldwide (2024), with subscription fees as the main revenue source

### 5. Promotion Mix
| Element | Highlights |
|---|---|
| Advertising | Hyper-targeted ads; Instagram Reels, YouTube trailers, OTT cross-promotions, billboards |
| Sales promotion | Free trials, mobile-only plans (India), telecom bundling (T-Mobile; Jio and Airtel), limited-time previews, referral programs |
| Public relations | Media coverage, cast publicity tours, awards, cultural conversation, fan community engagement, social media PR |
| Direct marketing | Personalized email, push notifications, AI-based recommendations |
| Digital and social media | Celebrity promotions, regional content marketing, video content marketing |
| Experiential marketing | Immersive real-life brand experiences, such as the *One Piece* premiere drone light show |

The deck also covers Netflix's promotion budget (about $1.11 billion on sales and marketing in one quarter of 2025).

## Key Takeaways

- Netflix differentiates through **original, localized content** rather than relying only on licensed titles.
- **Data and personalization** shape both content creation and the user experience.
- **Tiered and region-specific pricing** (such as mobile-only plans in India) helps it reach price-sensitive markets.
- Promotion blends **global brand consistency with local cultural relevance**, including regional celebrities and content.
